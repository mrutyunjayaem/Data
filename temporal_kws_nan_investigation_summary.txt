1.  PROBLEM SUMMARY

During temporal keyword-spotting (KWS) stream processing, generated
frame-level NPZ files contained NaN values.

Initial inspection: - dev_009: rms_db had 36,440 NaNs out of 121,985
frames (about 29.87%). - dev_019: rms_db had 91,286 NaNs out of 122,530
frames (about 74.5%). - blank and entropy had 84 NaNs in dev_009 and 73
in dev_019. - log_delta had 1 NaN in each stream. - The score arrays
were otherwise finite. - rms_db NaNs formed a continuous tail: -
dev_009: first invalid frame 85,545; last valid frame 85,544. - dev_019:
first invalid frame 31,244; last valid frame 31,243.

This suggested a non-finite waveform sample was entering the cumulative
squared-amplitude calculation.

2.  RELEVANT NOTEBOOK IMPLEMENTATION

Notebook parameters: - Sample rate SR = 16,000 Hz - Frame hop HOP = 160
samples - Window WIN = 400 samples

The RMS calculation in StreamAudio.__getitem__ is:

    sq = np.r_[0.0, np.cumsum(wav.numpy().astype(np.float64)**2)]
    end = np.minimum(start + WIN, wav.numel())
    rms_db = 10 * np.log10(
        (sq[end] - sq[start]) /
        np.maximum(end - start, 1) + 1e-10
    )

A NaN/Inf waveform sample can make the cumulative sum non-finite from
that point onward, consistent with the continuous rms_db NaN tails.

The relevant synth_stream logic is:

    speech = load_audio(c["path"])

6.  ROOT-CAUSE CONCLUSION

Confirmed: - Both problematic speech WAV files exist but are empty. -
Each is 44 bytes and contains zero audio frames. - Their associated
noise files are valid and non-empty. - load_audio() correctly returns an
empty tensor for these files. - speech.pow(2).mean() on an empty tensor
evaluates to NaN. - clamp_min(1e-10) does not turn NaN into a finite
value. - The mixing scale therefore becomes NaN, contaminating the
synthesized waveform. - The non-finite waveform then contaminates the
cumulative RMS calculation and explains the NaN tail in rms_db.

The immediate cause is confirmed: zero-length speech WAV files are
entering stream synthesis. This is a data-integrity issue, not evidence
of a model prediction failure.

7.  WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN

The investigation has NOT established why these WAV files are empty.

Possible causes to test, not yet confirmed: - The original source
archive may contain empty audio entries. - Files may have become empty
during extraction or copying. - A preprocessing step may have created or
truncated them. - There may be more empty WAV files in the extracted
audio directory.

The original archive has not yet been checked for these entries. The
full directory has not yet been scanned to count all empty WAV files.
